A few things to know

Are similar products always the exact same item?

No. Similar results may have different models, colors, sizes, conditions, or regional specifications. Check the retailer listing and specify your preferred variant before paying.

What is included in the total?

Checkout shows the item price, service fee, and shipping separately. Test checkout uses illustrative fees and collects no money. Live checkout requires the merchant to configure actual delivery and service fees.

What happens if an item sells out after I pay?

The team checks availability before purchasing. If it cannot be purchased, the team contacts you and arranges a refund through the payment provider. Refunds are not automatically issued by this prototype.
